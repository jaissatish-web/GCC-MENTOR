## Implementation recommendations and test contracts

1. All legal tools need regime/worker category, termination date, contract/reason and pay-basis selectors before formulas. Unsupported combinations return unavailable, never another country's result.
2. Reasonable first implementation candidates after additional authority review: Saudi EOSB ordinary expiry/employer termination (exclude Art80/probation), resignation factors with Art87 exceptions; UAE federal ordinary full-time EOSB excluding savings/pensions; Qatar minimum statutory EOSB excluding dismissal/retirement substitutions.
3. Salary/dates arithmetic is independently implementable with explicitly user-entered divisors and no legal-entitlement claim. Service duration is calendar arithmetic, not credited-service adjudication.
4. Reference arithmetic fixtures (derived, not official worked examples): Saudi ordinary5years×half of10,000=25,000;6years=35,000. Saudi resignation at exactly5years factor1/3, above5 below10 factor2/3;2years threshold,10years full. UAE one full eligible year at basic3,000 and confirmed30day basis→2,100;5years→10,500;6→13,500. Label these engineering fixtures until independently official calculator checked.
5. Mandatory boundary tests: zero/negative/huge salaries; service just below/at1,2,5,10years; leap-year and fractions conventions; unpaid absence excludes threshold service; probation/dismissal/savings exclusions; before/after legal effective dates; impossible dates and missing jurisdiction. Round display currency2decimals only; retain calculation precision. Do not state universal legal rounding method without authority.
6. No six-country complete legal certification is established. Record per-rule source/effective dates/coverage and use the explicit blockers above; do not mark entire country verified from one article.

## Follow-up: narrow implementation coverage now supported

### UAE divisor/effective date resolved
Official consolidated download: https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1541/download
Article67 explicitly uses Gregorian year365days/month30days. Official federal-law PDF hosted by u.ae also supplies Article74 effective2February2022: https://assets.u.ae/api/public/content/eb65a60bbff949fa912eddd566613014?v=7174ed78 . Search extraction from these official documents succeeded even where direct HTML returned403. Article68 has historical indefinite-contract transition; safest first supported calculation requires employment commencement on/after2Feb2022, ordinary federal full-time foreign worker, no alternative savings/pension scheme, current ordinary termination, confirmed credited service excluding unpaid absence. Then B/30×(21×min(Y,5)+30×max(Y−5,0)), capped24B, zero below1eligible year. User-entered confirmed credited years avoids inclusive-day disagreements; document no inference from contract date alone. Current publication is supported by official u.ae current EOSB guidance; no need to block ordinary covered UAE formula solely over divisor.

### Saudi day/calendar convention: avoid false Gregorian default
Official definitions/current HRSD: https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%AA/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AD%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9

Narrow engine should take user-confirmed credited service years (optional decimal) explicitly established under applicable contract calendar, not derive legal years from Gregorian dates. Formula uses service years directly. Require covered Labour Law worker, no probation/Article80 dismissal, confirmed EOSB wage incl applicable actual-wage elements and Article86 exclusions, current ordinary exit. Can offer ordinary employer termination/expiry full base and ordinary resignation reductions; special exception reasons route to unavailable or separately sourced Art87full entitlement.
