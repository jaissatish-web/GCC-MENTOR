# Agreed product plan

Updated 22 October 2026. Owner: Satish. This records agreed direction; `CURRENT_STATE.md` records implementation evidence.

## Product and audience
A practitioner-led career preparation platform for people seeking work in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait and Bahrain. Support first-time applicants, early-career candidates, Gulf returners and people already in the Gulf. The product must not assume Indian nationality; country and profession requirements need contextual treatment. Current repository branding is GCC Mentor. ReadyGulf was discussed; a rename/domain migration is not authorized by this document.

## Agreed journey
1. Upload or paste a resume, or use manual entry.
2. Extract facts once, reuse known answers, show what was captured and what needs correction. Ask only for missing or uncertain information; avoid duplicate contact/WhatsApp questions.
3. Show understandable, distinct Profile Readiness and Gulf preparation results, with actionable gaps. Public results may reveal a limited preview; signup gives the full workspace.
4. Carry the same draft into review. User confirms facts before Career Profile creation. Keep incomplete details visible and easy to repair.
5. One Career Profile supports a base resume, readable templates, job-specific optimization, cover letters, Q&A and repeated mock interview practice with useful reports/progress. LinkedIn and NaukriGulf preparation are additional direct services.

## Product rules
- Ground outputs in candidate-provided facts. Explain uncertainty and extraction failures clearly.
- Readiness is preparation guidance, not a visa eligibility decision or hiring probability. Age, nationality and purchases do not earn readiness points.
- Support all six destinations and an open-to-all choice. Passport status/expiry, present situation, availability and applicable professional preparation matter; do not store passport numbers.
- Consistent mobile-first navigation, readable reports and shared typography. Preserve rotating resume/globe experiences unless a later explicit decision changes them.
- Preserve service logic in a design-only task; protect template typography from app-wide typography changes.

## Working sequence
Discuss → research where useful → record the finalized requirement and acceptance criteria → implement → review/test → record actual status and next action. Do not silently turn a proposal into approved work. The founder explains desired experience; the assistant translates it into specifications and coordinates implementation.
