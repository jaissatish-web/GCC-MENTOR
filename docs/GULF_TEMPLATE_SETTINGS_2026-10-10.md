# Gulf template presentation and settings

Resume preview controls are grouped under Template settings. Text settings and Photo settings are independently expandable and start collapsed. PhotoUpload stays mounted when hidden, preserving an in-flight upload and its existing immediate-save behaviour. Photo permissions, sizes, visibility, style state, save handlers and download gates are unchanged.

All 50 registry display names now use neutral Gulf or GCC names. Profession recommendations and profession filters are removed from the chooser UI; every available template is shown. Existing field/category metadata, template IDs, versions, renderers, layouts, availability and photo/style capabilities are preserved. Already saved CVs continue using their same designs. The landing template orbit reads the shared names automatically.

A short Your Gulf career workspace line appears with the app wordmark, with brief Gulf context on the dashboard, profile, gallery, target-job and preparation screens. No extra explanatory sections or job-outcome promises are introduced.
