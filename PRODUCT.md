## Founder-approved principles

GCC Mentor helps professionals prepare for a desired job in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait or Bahrain. It serves people seeking their first Gulf role and people already in the region pursuing progression or a change of employer, project, role or industry.

One truthful Career Profile supports multiple independent target-job preparation journeys. A commissioning professional can prepare separately for a maintenance role and a lead role. Each journey can highlight different verified experience and transferable skills while preserving the same employment, education and qualification facts. Show genuine gaps and unknowns before suggesting what to learn, verify or practice. Never invent candidate facts or personal work incidents. Hypothetical practice must be labelled.

The scope is career preparation. Hiring outcomes, ATS vendor behaviour, immigration eligibility and employer endorsement are outside the product's guarantees.

## Implemented journey

The landing page introduces the public Gulf Readiness Scorecard, Free Tools and the signed-in workspace. A visitor can start Gulf Readiness before signup. Target-job match is part of the signed-in Resume Optimizer flow. The candidate can upload or paste a resume, review extracted details, or enter information directly. Saving confirmed data builds the Career Profile. Profile completeness, Gulf preparedness and job fit answer different questions.

The candidate can create a base resume from the Career Profile or start a separate target by supplying a role and job description. The job package groups the optimized resume, cover letter, interview preparation and tracking for that target. The Resume Library brings saved packages together. Mock Interview supports recurring practice and progress review. LinkedIn and NaukriGulf draft preparation use related profile and job context.
